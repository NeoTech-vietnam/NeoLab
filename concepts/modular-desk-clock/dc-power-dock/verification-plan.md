## 1. Entry conditions and evidence rules

Before each test, freeze its numerical acceptance limits from the PRD target register and actual component/pack data. If a limit is TBD, record the test as characterization or blocked, never passed. Begin fault characterization using a current-limited supply and battery emulator; test a real approved pack only within its permitted conditions and an appropriate lab procedure.

Identify DUT serial, PCB/mechanical/interface revisions, firmware/build and configuration, approved pack model/age/health, adapter/cable, receiver revision or electronic-load model, instrument settings/calibration status and ambient conditions. Keep raw oscilloscope captures, load scripts/settings, meter/thermal logs, photographs, calculations, anomalies and the resulting decision. A waveform screenshot without its voltage/current scales and timebase is insufficient.

Separate evidence types: source/datasheet review, calculation, simulation, bench emulation, real clock test, mechanical test and external compliance report. Simulation and fixture results inform design; real assembled-clock measurements remain required for runtime and integration acceptance.

Proposed evidence location after product source location is decided: `products/<chosen-product>/tests/test-reports/<date>-<revision>/`. This is a proposed future path, not an existing test report. Each result records requirement IDs, test ID, method, expected limits, observed values, raw evidence paths, verdict and reviewer. All T01–T20 below currently have **no execution evidence**.

## 2. Minimum bench capability

- Programmable/current-limited DC input and supported USB-C sources/cables; CC/PD analyzer or equivalent to verify actual input contract.
- Electronic load with dynamic profiles, plus the real core/display when available.
- Battery simulator with suitable source/sink behavior for charger tests; approved pack for permitted real-use tests.
- Oscilloscope with appropriate voltage/current probing; measure receiver voltage at the mating interface and cell current separately. Record bandwidth, probe grounding and sampling for ripple.
- DC power/energy measurement, thermocouples or thermal camera with suitable surface/emissivity checks, connector resistance measurement.
- Connector coupons, controlled mating/cycle fixture, mechanical mass/CG mockup, retention/cable-force fixtures and assembly tools.

Do not order a production pack or final connector solely to populate this list. Choose evaluation parts after G0/G1 inputs are established.

## 3. Test matrix

| Test | Requirements | Method and required observations | Acceptance / gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| T01 — Input qualification | PWR-01 | Both USB-C plug orientations; every advertised source level and supported PD profile if used; attach/detach; weak source and long/high-resistance cable. Log detected contract, programmed input limit and actual current. Check safe power-on defaults before supervisor configuration. | Never exceed qualified source/cable allowance; higher voltage only after supported negotiation; defined fallback/status. G1/G4. |
| T02 — Power budget and DC regulation | PWR-01, PWR-02, PWR-04 | Sweep input range/current allowance, no-load to rated continuous load, battery high/nominal/near-cutoff and absent. Measure input/output/cell power, rail drop and converter efficiency. Add worst simultaneous load plus requested charge. | Rail meets frozen window; charging reduces before source overload; actual battery supplementation or mode refusal matches policy. G1/G4. |
| T03 — Dynamic load and contact losses | PWR-02, IFC-03 | Apply measured boot/display/radio/alarm profiles as applicable; sweep receiver capacitance and load rise time. Capture receiver rail, inrush, ripple, overshoot and cell/input current. Repeat hot and after contact aging with maximum harness resistance. | Voltage/ripple/peak duration limits met at receiver, contact heating/drop within allocated budget. No reset. G1/G2/G4. |
| T04 — Adapter transitions | PWR-03, REL-03 | Plug/unplug at high, mid and low usable battery charge, idle and maximum load, charging active and input-limited. Proposed screen: 100 transitions across matrix, recording both rail and receiver reset/log signals. | No reset or out-of-envelope event in supported seamless cases. Depleted/absent battery follows defined shutdown/start behavior. G1/G4. |
| T05 — Charging and allocation | PWR-04, PWR-05, BAT-01, UX-01 | Use battery simulator first; observe precharge, CC/CV, termination/recharge, changing system load and adapter limits. Confirm charger defaults and exact cell voltage/current settings. Follow with permitted real-pack charge cycle; time recharge with the defined operating and idle profiles. | Chosen pack limits and recharge-time targets met; termination not confused by system load; status distinguishes charging, full, inhibited and net discharge. Adequate-adapter no-battery operation is stable. G1/G4. |
| T06 — Empty/missing battery and recovery | PWR-05, PWR-08, BAT-04, SYS-02 | Emulate UV threshold, hysteresis and recovery; missing/protection-tripped/aged high-resistance pack; source reconnect near boot threshold. Measure restart count and warning/shutdown sequence. Test core time recovery after complete power loss. | No damaging deep discharge or repeated reboot loop; predictable restart and documented RTC/time behavior. Approved packs only; do not force a real cell below limits. G1/G4. |
| T07 — Load faults and inrush | PWR-06, IFC-02, SYS-01 | Using controlled fault fixtures: output short, overload ramps/pulses, large capacitance, intermittent short/contact bounce and simulated regulator overvoltage. | T09 — Reverse power and signaling | PWR-07, IFC-01, IFC-04 | Receiver independently energized as bounded misuse; USB input absent; DATA/DET driven with each side unpowered; communication loss, bus stuck, invalid identity and stale budget. | T10 — Desk, sleep and storage | PWR-08, UX-01, REL-03 | Proposed 24-hour real desk profile including dim/sleep, no load, alarms if included and charging. ## 4. Operating-state matrix

| State / event | Required behavior to design and verify |
|---|---|
| No source, no usable battery | Output off; safe low leakage; core recovery after total power loss is documented. |
| Adapter present, receiver absent | Main output off; charging only within qualified input and pack limits; low-energy detection may remain active. |
| Valid receiver and adequate adapter | Controlled ramp; power receiver first, allocate remaining power to charging. |
| Adapter insufficient | Reduce charge first; indicate actual net battery direction; supplement only within defined policy, otherwise reduce/reject load. |
| Adapter removed, usable battery | Continue within accepted rail envelope; update status without reset. |
| Battery low | Notify and shed optional loads where possible; safe cutoff with hysteresis. |
| Battery missing, adequate adapter | Stable supported operation and clear no-battery status; no portability promise. |
| NTC invalid / pack outside temperature limits | Inhibit charging or discharge as specified by pack/protection policy; visible status and safe recovery. |
| Receiver partial, unknown or removed | Main output stays off or disables safely; no unrestricted power during probing. |
| Output short / overtemperature | Hardware protection acts within validated energy/time limits; bounded recovery without heating/reboot oscillation. |
| Supervisor reset or data failure | Safe source/current defaults and autonomous protection; no unrestricted output from stale settings. |
| User services battery/contact board | Unplug, power down and disconnect pack per procedure; reassemble and run functional checks before use. |

## 5. Gate reporting

Use one row per requirement/test result with `Not run`, `Characterized`, `Pass`, `Fail` or `Blocked` and evidence links. Do not turn “planned”, “datasheet-supported”, or a matched configuration into “Pass”. Carry open failures into the next gate with an owner and disposition; critical safety/rail faults prevent progression to real-user use. At G4, rerun relevant tests after any component, firmware, pack, connector or mechanical change that affects the result.
